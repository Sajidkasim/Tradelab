Your lab and your privacy

Updated 30 October 2026

PrimusLab is a private strategy tester operated by Sajid Kasim. This page explains how this app uses your information.

Signing in

Google and Supabase handle Google sign-in. PrimusLab requests your basic profile and verified email to identify your account and open your lab. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or your brokerage account. Your Google password is entered only on Google's own sign-in page.

Your lab data

The app stores the trades, CSV contents, tags, notes and preferences you choose to save. Each account has separate lab storage. Other members cannot view your lab data through the app. The site owner can see account email addresses, joining and sign-in times, recent website activity, active app sessions, browser/device hints reported by your browser and connection IP addresses recorded by the server, and can block access. Browser hints can be inaccurate; an IP address may belong to a VPN or hosting proxy. PrimusLab does not collect Wi-Fi names, personal phone numbers or precise location. It does not perform IP geolocation lookups. Visible signed-in pages report activity about once a minute. Session details are removed on sign-out or expiry cleanup; the latest account activity/device details remain with your account until account removal. This account list does not show lab contents. The service operator and hosting providers retain infrastructure access; labs are not end-to-end encrypted.

Your daily Journal

Your daily notes, trade cards and screenshot references are stored in your private workspace. Screenshots are compressed and stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. The app checks your signed-in workspace before serving an image; it does not create public image links. Deleting a Journal entry moves it to Deleted entries, where you can restore it. Deleted entries and their screenshots remain stored for recovery. Removing a screenshot detaches it from the entry; it does not immediately erase the stored file. Deleting a Journal entry does not delete trades already saved in Strategy Lab.

Where information is processed

Render hosts the website, Supabase provides authentication and database storage, and Cloudflare R2 stores Journal screenshots. These providers and Google also process information needed to deliver and secure their services, under their own privacy policies. PrimusLab does not sell your lab data or provide an advertising feed.

Sessions and browser storage

A required browser cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Sign out revokes that app session. The browser may remember interface preferences such as your theme. Google access tokens are not stored in the browser by PrimusLab.

Your controls

Download your lab data from My lab → Backup & restore. Delete individual trades or use Manage trades → Delete all trades with its count confirmation. Records remain stored until you delete them or request account removal; hosting providers may retain technical logs or recovery copies according to their own policies. Contact the operator for account removal or privacy questions.
