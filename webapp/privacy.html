Your journal and your privacy

Updated 29 October 2026

PrimusLab is a private trading research journal operated by Sajid Kasim. This page explains how this app uses your information.

Signing in

Google and Supabase handle Google sign-in. PrimusLab requests your basic profile and verified email to identify your account and open your journal. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or your brokerage account. Your Google password is entered only on Google's own sign-in page.

Your journal

The app stores the trades, CSV contents, tags, notes, accounts and preferences you choose to save. Each account has separate journal storage. Other members cannot view your journal through the app. The site owner can see account email addresses, joining and sign-in times, recent website activity, active app sessions, browser/device hints reported by your browser and connection IP addresses recorded by the server, and can block access. Browser hints can be inaccurate; an IP address may belong to a VPN or hosting proxy. PrimusLab does not collect Wi-Fi names, personal phone numbers or precise location. It does not perform IP geolocation lookups. Visible signed-in pages report activity about once a minute. Session details are removed on sign-out or expiry cleanup; the latest account activity/device details remain with your account until account removal. This account list does not show journal contents. The service operator and hosting providers retain infrastructure access; journals are not end-to-end encrypted.

Where information is processed

Render hosts the website and Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Google, Render and Supabase also process information needed to deliver and secure their services, under their own privacy policies. PrimusLab does not sell your journal data or provide an advertising feed.

Sessions and browser storage

A required browser cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Sign out revokes that app session. The browser may remember interface preferences such as your theme. Google access tokens are not stored in the browser by PrimusLab.

Your controls

Download your journal from My journal → Backup & restore. Delete individual trades or use Manage trades → Delete all trades with its count confirmation. Records remain stored until you delete them or request account removal; hosting providers may retain technical logs or recovery copies according to their own policies. Contact the operator for account removal or privacy questions.
